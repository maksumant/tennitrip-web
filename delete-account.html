TenniTrip Support

Delete your TenniTrip account

You can permanently delete your TenniTrip account and the data associated with it. The easiest way is from inside the app. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email.

Request deletion by email

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to TenniTrip.
  2. Open your Profile or Settings.
  3. Find the Account section and choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when TenniTrip asks you to confirm.

This action is permanent and signs you out after the account has been deleted.

If you cannot access the app

Email contact@tennitrip.app from the email address associated with your TenniTrip account and ask us to delete the account.

We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder before processing it. Never send us your password or authentication codes.

What is deleted

Deleting the account removes the account and the TenniTrip data that belongs only to that account, including its player planning data such as tournaments, packing lists, travel, spending, notes and results.

Deletion cannot be undone. Signing in again later creates a new account and does not restore deleted account data.

Shared data and limited retention

Information that has been legitimately shared with another TenniTrip account may remain with that other account where it belongs to their own planning or collaboration record.

We do not retain deleted account data for ordinary product use. Limited records may be retained only where necessary to meet legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution obligations, and only for as long as that purpose requires.

For more information about how TenniTrip handles personal information, see our Privacy Policy. For other help, visit TenniTrip Support.
