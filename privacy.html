Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 August 2026

TenniTrip helps tennis players and families plan tournaments, travel and related activities. This Privacy Policy explains what information TenniTrip may collect, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, TenniTrip may process:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, account identifier and authentication information supplied through our sign-in provider.
  • Player information that you choose to add, such as a player's name, age or birth year, city and tennis-related profile information.
  • Tournament and participation information, including tournaments being considered or entered, age categories, registration status, withdrawal information, tournament dates and results.
  • Planning information, such as packing items, travel plans, booking or reference information, expenses and notes that you choose to save.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate and protect the service, such as authentication tokens, request information and limited diagnostic or security records generated by our infrastructure.

TenniTrip does not ask users to provide information that is unnecessary for the feature they are using.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate TenniTrip, authenticate users, save and synchronize tournament plans, display relevant tournament and travel information, maintain account and data security, diagnose service problems, improve reliability, and support features requested by users.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising.

3. Junior players

TenniTrip is designed to support tennis families and may contain information about junior players. A parent, guardian or other authorized adult should manage information entered about a child where required by applicable law. Users should only enter information about another person when they are authorized to do so, and should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information in free-text notes.

TenniTrip does not knowingly use junior-player information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Service providers

TenniTrip relies on service providers to operate the app. These may include cloud hosting and database services, authentication services, app distribution and platform services, and tournament or other data providers used by a feature. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to TenniTrip and are expected to protect information appropriately.

Our current infrastructure includes Amazon Web Services for backend, database and authentication-related services. Sign-in may also involve identity providers selected by the user, such as Google. Apple and Expo may process technical information as part of app distribution, build or platform services.

5. Data sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate TenniTrip, when a user directs us to do so, or when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or others. We do not sell personal information to data brokers, advertisers or other third parties.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain account and planning information while it is needed to provide TenniTrip and for a reasonable period needed for security, backup, legal or operational purposes. Individual planning records may be removed through features provided by the app where available.

Before TenniTrip is offered for general App Store distribution, users who can create an account will be provided a clear way to initiate deletion of their account and associated personal data. Information that must be retained for legal or security reasons may be kept only for the period required for that purpose.

7. Your choices

You can choose what planning information you add to TenniTrip and can update or remove supported records in the app. Where a device permission is used, such as notifications in a future version, you can control that permission through your device settings. You may also stop using TenniTrip at any time.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, including authenticated access and encrypted network connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect access to their devices and accounts.

9. International processing

TenniTrip and its service providers may process information in locations where they operate infrastructure. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to use service providers and safeguards appropriate to the information being processed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TenniTrip develops or our data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel where required.

11. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions, requests, or other support related to this policy, contact TenniTrip at contact@tennitrip.app. TenniTrip is currently in beta development, and an in-app account-deletion flow will be provided before external/public distribution.
