Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 August 2026

TenniTrip helps tennis players and families organize tournaments, travel, expenses, preparation and match records. This Privacy Policy explains what information TenniTrip processes, why we use it, how it is handled, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, TenniTrip may process the following information:

  • Account information, including your name, email address and identifiers received through our authentication providers.
  • Player information that you choose to add, currently including a player's name and birth year.
  • Tournament and participation information, including events added or selected, age categories, registration status, entry and withdrawal deadlines, withdrawal records and tournament participation history.
  • Travel information, including flights, hotels, local transport, dates and times, status, and booking or reference numbers that you choose to enter.
  • Packing information, including packing-list items and whether they have been packed.
  • Expense information, including category, description, amount, currency and date spent.
  • Notes that you choose to write about tournament planning, practice, matches or other matters.
  • Match and result information, including draw, round, match format, participants, score and match status.
  • Technical and security information necessary to authenticate requests, operate the service, protect accounts and diagnose failures. This can include authentication tokens and limited server/request records.

TenniTrip does not require users to provide information that is unnecessary for the feature they are using. Please avoid placing unnecessary sensitive personal information in free-text notes.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate TenniTrip, authenticate users, save and synchronize tournament plans, calculate planning reminders and readiness information, show tournament and travel details, maintain a player's tournament history, provide weather information where available, secure the service, diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising.

3. Junior players

TenniTrip is intended to support tennis families and may therefore contain information about junior players. A parent, guardian or other authorized adult should manage a child's information where required by applicable law. Users should only enter information about another person when they are authorized to do so.

TenniTrip does not sell junior-player information or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Authentication and sign-in

TenniTrip currently supports sign-in through Google using Amazon Cognito as the authentication service. When you sign in, TenniTrip receives the account information needed to identify your TenniTrip account, such as your name, email address and authentication identifier. TenniTrip does not receive your Google password.

Your Google account remains controlled by Google. Deleting a TenniTrip account does not delete your Google account.

5. Tournament, location and weather providers

TenniTrip may obtain public tournament information from tennis organizations or tournament providers, including AITA. To support tournament-location and weather features, a tournament's location may also be processed through geocoding and weather services. These features use the tournament location; TenniTrip does not currently use your phone's precise GPS location for this purpose.

6. Service providers

TenniTrip relies on service providers to operate the app. Our current infrastructure includes Amazon Web Services for application hosting, database services and authentication. Google may process information when you choose Google sign-in. Apple and Expo may process technical information as part of app build, distribution and platform services. Tournament, geocoding and weather providers may process the information needed for those features.

We provide service providers only the information reasonably needed for the applicable service.

7. Sharing between family members

TenniTrip's data model supports more than one authorized account having access to the same player. Where a player is shared with another authorized parent or guardian, information belonging to that shared player may remain available to the other authorized account even if one account is deleted. Deleting your account removes your access and deletes information that only your account can reach; it does not delete a shared player's records that another authorized account still manages.

8. Other data sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate TenniTrip, when a user directs us to do so, or when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or others. We do not sell personal information to advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

9. Account and record deletion

TenniTrip provides an in-app Delete account option in Settings. Account deletion is immediate and irreversible after confirmation.

When you delete your TenniTrip account, TenniTrip removes the account record and data that only that account can access. For a player managed only by your account, this includes that player's tournament participations and associated packing items, travel records, expenses, notes and match results. If another authorized account also manages a player, the shared player and their records remain for that account and only your account's link is removed.

TenniTrip's authentication-directory identity may remain after app-data deletion. If you later sign in again with the same Google identity, TenniTrip can create a new, empty TenniTrip account. Deleting your TenniTrip account does not delete your Google account or data independently controlled by Google.

You may also delete supported individual records, such as tournaments or player records, using the controls provided in the app.

10. Data retention

We retain account and planning information while your account is active and the information is needed to provide TenniTrip. When data is deleted through the app, it is removed from the active TenniTrip database in accordance with the implemented deletion flow. Limited information may still exist temporarily in infrastructure backups, logs or security records until those systems rotate or expire it, or where retention is required by law or necessary for security.

11. Your choices

You control much of the information stored in TenniTrip by choosing what player, tournament, travel, expense, note and result information to add. Supported records can be edited or deleted in the app. You can sign out at any time, and you can permanently delete your TenniTrip account from Settings.

If TenniTrip introduces device permissions such as push notifications, permission will be controlled through the app and your device settings.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. These include authenticated access, authorization checks that restrict accounts to permitted player and tournament data, and encrypted network connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect access to their devices and Google accounts.

13. International processing

TenniTrip and its service providers may process information in locations where they operate infrastructure. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to use service providers and safeguards appropriate to the information being processed.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TenniTrip develops or our data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel where required.

15. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions, account or data requests, or questions about this policy, contact contact@tennitrip.app.
