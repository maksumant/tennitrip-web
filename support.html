TenniTrip Support

Need help with TenniTrip? Contact us and we’ll help with account access, app issues, tournament planning features or general questions.

Email contact@tennitrip.app

Signing in

TenniTrip supports Google sign-in and Sign in with Apple. If sign-in does not complete, check your internet connection and try again. If the problem continues, email us with the device type and what happened.

Account and data

You can delete your TenniTrip account from the app’s Settings. Account deletion is irreversible. For privacy or data questions, contact us at the email above.

Tournaments and rankings

If tournament, ranking or player information looks incorrect, send us the relevant tournament or player details and a screenshot if possible. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.

Travel, packing and weather

If a planning feature is not behaving as expected, tell us what you were trying to do, which tournament you were viewing, and the screen where the problem occurred.

Coach collaboration

If a coach or family member cannot access a shared player or tournament suggestion, contact us with the account email addresses involved. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

Report a bug

The most useful bug report includes your device model, iOS or Android version, TenniTrip app version, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and a screenshot where appropriate.

For details about how TenniTrip handles personal information, see our Privacy Policy.
